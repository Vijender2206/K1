the folded-note detour

For once, I let
the paper do the talking.

Imagine a little note slipped across the café table. Folded twice. Extremely casual. Obviously.

Hey, you.

I had a better line planned. Something clever enough to make you roll your eyes, then smile when you thought I wasn’t looking.

But here’s the part I keep editing around: I like talking to you. The big stories, the tiny updates, the “nothing happened today” conversations that somehow become the best bit of the day.

And yes, I’d like a few more of those in person. Preferably across a café table, while you explain why stealing my fries is apparently different from ordering your own.

— the one who will order extra fries ♡

P.S. Are you a note in the margin? Because I came here for the story, but you’re the bit I keep going back to.

P.P.S. Yes, I practised folding this. Please act impressed.
