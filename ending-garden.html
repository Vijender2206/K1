the garden detour

I picked you a flower.
Then overthought it.

The florist said “say something sweet”. Unfortunately, I heard “give a presentation”.

Choose your flower. I’ve prepared remarks.

For once, the flowers aren’t the reason I’m smiling on this walk.

Somewhere between the flower debate and the snack stop, I stop rehearsing what to say. This is nicer.
