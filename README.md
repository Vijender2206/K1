# A little story, just for you

A static, illustrated Hinglish adventure with teasing banter, original pickup lines,
and a single relationship invitation. Open `index.html` or serve this folder with
`python -m http.server 4175 --bind 127.0.0.1`.

Story: letter → banter → late-night lines → chai break → choice of garden, rooftop,
or handwritten note → invitation → celebration. A separate friendly closing page
is also available at `later.html`.

The drink choice stays in the current browser tab and appears on the final date-idea
ticket. The playful No button dodges the cursor or taps, changes its teasing reply,
and eventually gives in, just like the original. Nothing is sent, booked, or collected.
No build step or dependencies are required. Google Fonts are optional; local fallback
fonts keep the pages usable offline. All illustrations and audio are generated locally
with SVG, CSS, and Web Audio.

The gift button beside the sound control opens `flowers.html`, a full-screen blooming
flower surprise adapted from M1. Replay the bloom or return to the previous story page.
Source attribution and the original license notices are in `licenses/`.
